This is the first entry in what will become a running collection of research write-ups.

The plan is simple: each time there's something worth writing down — a finding, a half-formed idea, a summary of a paper worth remembering — it goes here as a dated entry. Over time this page becomes a searchable record of the work rather than a single polished document that's always slightly out of date.

New articles are added by dropping a Markdown file into the `_posts` folder of the site's repository. Nothing more is required — the homepage picks it up automatically.
